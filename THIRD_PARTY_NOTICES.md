[assets/provenance.json](assets/provenance.json) records the immutable upstream
source or original design node and SHA-256 for every downloaded asset. Design
SVGs are exact exports captured 6 September 2026; their hashes pin the captured
bytes because the editable source may change. Fonts/icons are offline assets;
no runtime font or image download is required. Custom colors use a render-time
color filter without rewriting the SVG bytes.

`registerTroveAssetLicenses()` adds bundled font, icon and original-artwork
notices to Flutter's `LicenseRegistry` for `showLicensePage`. Preserve these
entries when connecting the product's About screen.
